E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behavior Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales and customer behavior. The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful insights related to customer engagement, purchases, revenue, cart abandonment, discounts, reviews, and website visits.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer browsing and purchasing behavior.
Understand cart additions and abandonment.
Compare revenue and discounts across session-duration groups.
Analyze customer ratings, reviews, and helpful votes.
Identify website visit patterns by month, weekday, and day.
Present insights through an interactive and visually appealing dashboard.

📈 Dashboard Features
🛍️ Customer Engagement

Analyzes pages viewed, products added to cart, cart abandonment, and quantity purchased.

💰 Sales & Revenue

Provides insights into purchases, quantity, revenue, and discount amounts.

⏱️ Session Analysis

Compares Short, Long, Very Short, and Very Long sessions with revenue and discount metrics.

⭐ Customer Reviews

Analyzes ratings, review activity, and helpful votes to understand customer feedback.

📅 Visit Analysis

Examines website activity based on month, weekday, and day.

The dashboard helps businesses understand:
